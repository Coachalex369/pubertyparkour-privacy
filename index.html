Puberty Parkour Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026 · Applies to version 0.5.0 and later, until updated

Puberty Parkour (“the game”) is published by Alexandria Ltd. (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game handles on Android in the United States.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in and no chat. The game itself works offline.
  • The game offers optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob. You only see an ad when you choose to watch one for an in-game reward: a revive, or bonus ThisGame Bucks.
  • When an ad is requested, Google’s ad software collects some device information (described below) to deliver the ad, measure it and prevent fraud. We do not run any servers that collect data from the game, and we do not sell personal information.
  • No real-money purchases.
  • The game is made for players aged 13 and over.

Information stored on your device

Your progress stays in the app’s private storage on your device: character look, outfits, stars, ThisGame Bucks (in-game currency with no real-money value), settings and best results. Android cloud backup is turned off for this app. Uninstalling the game deletes this data. We can’t see it.

Permissions

The game asks Android for:

  • Vibration, for optional haptic feedback (you can turn it off in Settings).
  • Internet and network state, used only to load optional rewarded ads.
  • Advertising ID and Android ad services (ad ID, attribution and topics), declared by Google’s ad software. The game requests non-personalised ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and turn off ad topics, at any time in Android settings.
  • Wake lock and foreground service, requested by Google’s ad software so ads play correctly.

The game does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob. An ad plays only when you tap a button to watch one, in two places: to revive after a crash, or for bonus ThisGame Bucks on the results screen. No ad ever plays on its own, and you never have to watch one. Ads are non-personalised, limited to content rated T (Teen) or lower, and requested with Google’s teen protections and restricted data processing for US state privacy laws. Where the law requires consent, Google’s consent message is shown before any ad is requested.

When an ad is requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares the following with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • how you interact with ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and ad views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app and ad performance; and
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

This data is encrypted in transit. It goes to Google, not to us. Google’s use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Age signals from Google Play

In some US states the law requires app stores to verify users’ ages and get parental approval for minors. Where Google Play makes this available, the game asks Google Play for:

  • the player’s age range (for example “13–15”), and
  • for supervised accounts, whether a parent has approved the app’s changes.

The game uses this only to switch off ads for players Google Play reports as under 13, players Google Play says still need age verification, and supervised teens whose parent hasn’t approved the app’s changes. If Google Play doesn’t provide an age signal, ads are still shown with the same teen protections as for every player. This information is kept only in memory while the game is running. It is never stored, never sent to us and never used for advertising. Google Play handles age verification itself; the game never asks for your birth date, ID documents or other personal details.

Purchases

The game offers no real-money purchases. If purchases are added, payments will be handled entirely by Google Play, and we will update this policy first.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Players Google Play reports as under 13 see no ads. If you are a parent and have a question about your child’s use of the game, contact us.

Teens aged 13 to 17 are part of the game’s intended audience. Ads shown to them are non-personalised and limited to content rated T (Teen) or lower. Parents of supervised Google accounts can manage installs and approvals through Google Family Link.

Data retention and deletion

We do not collect or keep any information about you ourselves. Your game progress exists only on your device; you can delete it at any time by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the game. Data collected by Google’s ad software is handled under Google’s retention practices; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Age-range information from Google Play is held in memory only while the game is running and is discarded when the game closes.

Your rights (US residents)

We hold no personal information about you on our own systems, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data collected by Google’s ad software, you can use your Android ad settings and Google’s privacy tools. You can contact us with any privacy question, and we will reply.

Changes

If the game’s data practices change, we will update this policy, and its effective date, before the change ships.
