Puberty Parkour Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026 · Applies to version 0.4.2 and later, until updated

Puberty Parkour (“the game”) is published by Alexandria Ltd. (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game handles on Android in the United States. It describes the current version first; features we plan to add later are listed separately under “Planned features”.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in and no chat. The game works offline.
  • We do not collect, receive, store, share or sell any personal information. We don’t run any servers that collect data from the game.
  • This version shows no ads, offers no purchases and contains no advertising or analytics software. It does not request internet access.
  • The game is made for players aged 13 and over.

Information stored on your device

Your progress stays in the app’s private storage on your device: character look, outfits, stars, ThisGame Bucks (in-game currency with no real-money value), settings and best results. Android cloud backup is turned off for this app. Uninstalling the game deletes this data. We can’t see it.

Permissions

The game asks Android for one permission: vibration, for optional haptic feedback (you can turn it off in Settings). It does not request internet access, location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Advertising

This version of the game shows no ads and contains no advertising software. It does not collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP-based location, or usage or diagnostic data. If we add ads in a later version, we will update this policy before that version is released.

Age signals from Google Play

In some US states the law requires app stores to verify users’ ages and get parental approval for minors. Where Google Play makes this available, the game asks Google Play for:

  • the player’s age range (for example “13–15”), and
  • for supervised accounts, whether a parent has approved the app’s changes.

The game uses this only to switch off optional features (such as ads or purchases, if a later version offers them) for players Google Play reports as under 13, or whose age still needs to be verified. It is kept only in memory while the game is running. It is never stored, never sent to us and never used for advertising. Google Play handles age verification itself; the game never asks for your birth date, ID documents or other personal details.

Purchases

This version offers no real-money purchases. If purchases are added, payments will be handled entirely by Google Play, and we will update this policy first.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you are a parent and have a question about your child’s use of the game, contact us.

Teens aged 13 to 17 are part of the game’s intended audience. The game treats them the same as every other player: nothing about them is collected, stored by us or shared. Parents of supervised Google accounts can manage installs and approvals through Google Family Link.

Data retention and deletion

We do not collect or keep any information about you, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Your game progress exists only on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the game. Age-range information from Google Play is held in memory only while the game is running and is discarded when the game closes.

Your rights (US residents)

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can contact us with any privacy question, and we will reply.

Planned features (not in this version)

We plan to add these in a future version. They are not part of the current version, and this policy will be updated, with a new effective date, before any version that includes them is released:

  • Optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob, shown only when a player chooses to watch one for an in-game reward. Ads would be non-personalised, limited to content rated T (Teen) or lower, and requested with Google’s teen protections. Players Google Play reports as under 13 would see no ads. AdMob collects some device information to deliver ads; the updated policy will describe exactly what.
  • Optional in-app purchases, handled entirely by Google Play.

Changes

If the game’s data practices change, we will update this policy, and its effective date, before the change ships.
